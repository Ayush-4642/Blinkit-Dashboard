****📊 RETAILIQ – BLINKIT BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD****

🚀 Interactive Business Analytics Platform for Customer, Delivery, Inventory & Marketing Intelligence

RetailIQ is a modern, enterprise-style Business Intelligence Dashboard inspired by Blinkit. The project transforms raw business data into actionable insights through interactive visualizations, KPI monitoring, customer analytics, delivery intelligence, inventory tracking, and marketing performance analysis.

Built as a complete end-to-end analytics project, RetailIQ combines data cleaning, business analysis, dashboard design, and web deployment into a single platform.
